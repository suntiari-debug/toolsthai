# Tools Thai Visual Direction

Tools Thai is a calm Thai SME business utility rather than a generic dashboard. The visual language pairs warm paper surfaces with deep teal workspaces, structural navy, and restrained terracotta details. The document editor should feel like a focused document studio: a clear input column, a framed paper preview, ledger-line texture, and strong financial hierarchy.

The Thai-first voice should be practical and reassuring. Small monospaced labels add operational precision, while Thai headings and financial totals carry the authority. Primary actions use teal or ink; terracotta stays a warm brand detail; saffron is reserved for meaningful financial outcomes.

The responsive experience retains the same hierarchy. On small screens the document preview becomes a self-contained paper stage, form cards remain comfortably spaced, and key actions remain visible without adding visual noise.
